# A Level Physics (9702) Paper 4: Practice Papers

Three practice papers in the layout of the 2025 Cambridge International A Level Physics Paper 4 (A Level Structured Questions), each with its own mark scheme.

| Paper | Question paper | Mark scheme |
|-------|----------------|-------------|
| 1 | [9702 Practice Paper 1.pdf](9702%20Practice%20Paper%201.pdf) | [mark scheme](9702%20Practice%20Paper%201%20-%20mark%20scheme.pdf) |
| 2 | [9702 Practice Paper 2.pdf](9702%20Practice%20Paper%202.pdf) | [mark scheme](9702%20Practice%20Paper%202%20-%20mark%20scheme.pdf) |
| 3 | [9702 Practice Paper 3.pdf](9702%20Practice%20Paper%203.pdf) | [mark scheme](9702%20Practice%20Paper%203%20-%20mark%20scheme.pdf) |

Each paper has 10 questions, 2 hours and 100 marks, and covers all 14 A Level topics (12 to 25). Questions open with routine parts, then test whether you really understand the physics: a formula that gives an impossible answer, a quantity that does not change when you expect it to, or a student's claim to correct.

## Building

The papers are generated by `tools/physics_papers/` (see `SPEC.md` there):

```
tools/physics_papers/get_arial.sh          # once: fetches Arial (needs curl and cabextract)
python3 tools/physics_papers/build.py      # all three papers and mark schemes
```

The build checks that every part's marks in the question paper match its mark scheme and that each paper totals 100.
