# Cambridge International A Level Physics (9702): Paper 4 Past Papers, 2020 onwards

A Level **Paper 4 (A Level Structured Questions)** from every exam series between Feb-March 2020 and May-June 2026.

| Folder | Contents |
|--------|----------|
| [`question-papers`](question-papers) | Question papers (49) |
| [`mark-schemes`](mark-schemes) | Mark schemes (49), one for each question paper |
| [`examiner-reports`](examiner-reports) | Examiner reports (18), one per exam series |

## File naming

`9702_<session><yy>_<type>[_<variant>].pdf`

| Part | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Session `m` / `s` / `w` | Feb-March / May-June / Oct-Nov |
| `yy` | Year, e.g. `24` = 2024 |
| `qp` / `ms` / `er` | Question paper / mark scheme / examiner report |
| Variant `41` to `44` | Paper 4 variants 1 to 4 |

Example: `9702_w24_qp_42.pdf` is the Oct-Nov 2024 Paper 4, variant 2 question paper.

## Coverage

- Feb-March has only variant `42`.
- May-June and Oct-Nov have variants `41`, `42` and `43`. Variant `44` starts in May-June 2025.
- Oct-Nov 2026 hasn't been sat yet.

Each examiner report covers every 9702 paper in its series. The Paper 4 comments are in
the sections headed "Paper 9702/41", "Paper 9702/42" and so on.

Two examiner reports are missing:

- **May-June 2020**: the exams were cancelled because of COVID-19, so Cambridge didn't publish a report.
- **May-June 2026**: the report wasn't available online when these files were collected.
